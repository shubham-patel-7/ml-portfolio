Engine Predictive Maintenance

Predict remaining useful life of turbofan engines on NASA C-MAPSS and raise an alert in time.

  • Random Forest, XGBoost and a 3-seed LSTM ensemble, split by engine to avoid leakage.
  • XGBoost RMSE 13.86; the LSTM ensemble is most accurate near failure (RMSE 3.99 within 30 cycles) and catches 23 of 25 real failures at a 30-cycle alert.
  • Paired bootstrap shows the overall model differences are not statistically clear, and the write-up says so.
  • Streamlit dashboard with replay slider and tunable alert thresholds.

Network Intrusion Detection (NSL-KDD)

Can a model trained on known attacks catch new ones? ML versus deep learning under distribution shift.

  • Logistic Regression, Random Forest, XGBoost and a PyTorch MLP on identical preprocessing, 3 seeds each.
  • Validation F1 of about 0.998 drops to 0.74 to 0.81 on the official test set, which holds 17 unseen attack types.
  • Models detect 71 to 81% of known attack types but only 31 to 42% of novel ones.
  • Upload a CSV of connection records in the Streamlit app and tune the alert threshold.

Document Q&A with RAG

Ask questions about PDFs and get answers with the page they came from, plus an evaluation that measures retrieval quality.

  • Hybrid retrieval: FAISS vectors and BM25 fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion.
  • 25 hand-labelled questions; hybrid MiniLM + BM25 puts the gold page in the top 3 for all 25 (Hit@3 1.00, MRR 0.95).
  • Pluggable answer backends: Claude, any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, or an offline extractive answerer.
  • Found and fixed a ranking bug the evaluation exposed, with a regression test.
